French press — validated
Mild Honey (Café Quindío): 20g:310g, 32 clicks, 88–90°C, single pour, skim at 3:00, plunge 3:10–3:30, yield ~260–270ml — validated consistently across multiple sessions
Mild Honey tolerates extra contact margin due to its inherent sweetness base: one session skimmed ~30s later (skim 3:40, plunge 3:50) and the result was still excellent — caramel/mascabo, red fruit, no bitterness
Berry Bloom (Café Quindío), first successful French press recipe: 25g:388g (ratio ~1:15.5), 89–90°C, 34–35 clicks (coarser than usual 32), single pour, gentle stir 20–30s, skim 2:30–2:50, plunge 2:40–3:00 (total contact ~3:00). Result: amargor ~2/10, astringencia nearly zero, profile similar to V60 but lighter body
Geisha (Café Quindío; azahar/flores/durazno maduro): same conservative approach as Berry Bloom — 25g:388g, 89–90°C, 34–35 clicks, single pour, gentle stir 20–30s, skim 2:30–2:50, plunge 2:40–3:00. Very successful, similar to V60, much better than a prior attempt with different parameters
Berry Bloom French press scaled to 1 cup (~250ml): 18g:280g (same ~1:15.5 ratio), 89.5°C, 34 clicks, same conservative process. Real total time to full plunge: 3:20 (the user's skim routinely runs 20–30s longer than standard — tolerated well, same as seen with Mild Honey). Real yield: 241g. Result: excellent, mora fresca envolvente like fruit straight off the tree, body similar to V60, balanced non-punchy acidity, barely perceptible bitterness. Confirms the conservative formula scales well across volumes (25g/388g and 18g/280g both validated)
Sweet Hour 03 (Ranch): 20g, 32 clicks, 320g water, 88–90°C, double-pour bloom (100g at 0:00, complete at 0:30), skim at 3:00, plunge 3:10–3:30, yield ~261ml — validated
Villa Dora Bourbon Amarillo (Brasil, Serra da Mantiqueira, natural, tueste medio+, notas chocolate/pasas/frambuesa/caramelo): 25g coffee, ~377g water, 92°C, 30–32 clicks, stir at 0:30, skim at 3:00, plunge 3:10–3:30 — validated, resulted in zero bitterness, chocolate/pasas and caramelo notes confirmed, frambuesa not yet confirmed (tasted while still too hot)
